Conference/Journal 2023

Drive2Gauss: FeedForward 4D Gaussian Reconstruction from Multimodal Generative Driving Videos

Anonymous Authors
Anonymous Institution
Code Dataset Checkpoints

Demo Video

Abstract

Generating reusable driving environments requires both controllable future observations and an explicit representation that supports rendering from different viewpoints, yet existing methods still recover explicit scenes from generated RGB videos through optimization for each sequence. To overcome these limitations, we present Drive2Gauss, a feedforward framework that converts jointly generated RGB, metric depth, and dynamic flow into dynamic 4D Gaussian scenes. A Multimodal Video Generator predicts the three modalities from reference multiview images and future control signals. Furthermore, we introduce a Gaussian Query Decoder that uses depth to initialize sparse 3D queries and flow to associate the queries across time, and integrates appearance information through spatiotemporal feature aggregation and iterative query refinement. The reconstruction stage operates in a single feedforward pass without optimization for each scene. Extensive results on nuScenes demonstrate that our approach attains an FID of 6.38 and an FVD of 12.43 for future RGB generation, a PSNR of 29.91 dB when reconstructing its generated observations, and supports both direct rendering under lateral camera shifts and downstream video fixing.

Method Overview

Multimodal Video Generation

Joint RGB, depth, and dynamic-flow generation across six surround-view cameras.

Multimodal Dataset

Ground-truth RGB, metric depth, and dynamic flow across six surround-view cameras.

Feedforward 4D Gaussian Reconstruction

Generated observations are converted into renderable 4D Gaussian scenes in a single feedforward pass.

Novel-View Rendering

DGGT and our method render novel views from the same generated video input.

Application to Video Fixing

The reconstructed 4D Gaussian scene provides geometric guidance for restoring a degraded generated video.
